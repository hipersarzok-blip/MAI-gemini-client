# mAI

**mAI** is a fast, customizable AI chat and roleplay platform designed to give users complete control over how they interact with artificial intelligence.

## Purpose of the Application

Most standard AI assistants force users into a single, rigid conversational tone. **mAI** was built to solve this by combining three core experiences into one clean, fast interface:

1. **Everyday AI Assistant:** Get instant, accurate help with daily questions, coding, brainstorming, and problem-solving using ultra-fast Gemini models (`Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite`, `Gemini 3.8 Flash`, and `Gemini Flash`).
2. **Immersive Roleplay Engine:** Bring stories and characters to life. Choose from over 120 pre-built anime and fictional personas—or create your own custom character from scratch with unique personalities, emotional flaws, scenarios, and speech styles.
3. **Custom Directives & Expert Personas:** Shape the AI's behavior for specific disciplines (software engineering, medicine, finance, academic research, creative writing) or write your own custom system instructions that the AI strictly follows throughout the conversation.
